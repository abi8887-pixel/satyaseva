The Heart of Our Vocation

The Four Pillars of Our Life

Rooted in the charism of our Foundress, Mother Regina Woroniecka, and devoted to Truth (Satya) and Loving Service (Seva).

🙏

Eucharistic Prayer

Daily Holy Mass, Eucharistic Adoration, and the Liturgy of the Hours form the spiritual fountain from which all our community apostolate flows.

📖

Catechesis & Truth

Teaching Catholic faith, preparing children and families for sacraments, and building active basic Christian parish communities.

🏘️

Fraternal Community

Living together in Gospel simplicity, sharing meals and recreation, and supporting each other in deep sisterly affection across cultures.

❤️

Compassionate Service

Loving service to the poorest families, village children, foster girls, widows, and senior persons in rural mission stations.

Spiritual Growth

Journey of Religious Formation

A gradual and loving path of deepening intimacy with Jesus Christ and understanding religious vows.

1
6 Months – 1 Year

Aspirancy ("Come & See")

Live with our Sisters, participate in community prayer and daily apostolic life while discerning God's voice in your heart.

2
1 – 2 Years

Postulancy

Deeper spiritual grounding, catechism and Bible study, language learning, and active participation in family apostolate.

3
2 Canonical Years

Novitiate

An intense period of contemplative prayer, study of the Evangelical Counsels (Chastity, Poverty, Obedience), and spiritual transformation at Marianiketan Motherhouse.

4
5 – 6 Years

Juniorate (Temporary Vows)

First religious profession of vows. Sisters are assigned to mission stations, higher academic education, or healthcare and teaching ministry.

5
Perpetual Consecration

Final Profession of Vows

Forever consecrated to Christ as a full member of the Satyaseva Sisters, wearing the golden cross and ring of spousal devotion to the Lord.

Our Story

From one sister's calling,
to a family of many

I.The Seed — 1976

A Missionary's Calling

Our congregation began in 1976 in the Diocese of Mysore, Karnataka, founded by Rev. Mother Regina Woroniecka — a Polish missionary sister of the Ursulines of the Roman Union, formed in the spirituality of St. Angela Merici.

Read Mother Regina's Biography
Mother Regina Woroniecka
II.Taking Root

A Charism Takes Shape

The name Satyaseva — "Service to Truth" — captures what took root in those first years: a commitment to bring the Good News into the ordinary life of the family, through home visits, biblical prayer, and quiet catechetical teaching rather than the classroom alone.

Early community life, around 1977
III.Reaching Upward — 1987

A Generalate in Bengaluru

In 1987 the congregation's Generalate was established on Cookson Road, Richard's Town, Bengaluru — still our motherhouse today. New communities followed: Venkateshpuram (1987), Kadusonnappanahalli (1993), Bharathinagar (2002), and a home for orphaned children on Brigade Road (2004).

The Generalate, Richard's Town, Bengaluru
IV.A Season of Testing

Small, and Steady

Every young congregation faces lean years — fewer vocations than hoped, communities that had to wait for resources, a mission that outgrew its hands. Through those trials, the sisters drew strength from the Eucharist and from one another, walking miles on foot to attend daily Mass and never ceasing their catechetical visits to families in need.

With children in Mariapura, early 1980s
V.Today

A Family Across Two Continents

Nearly five decades on, the Satyaseva Sisters serve in dioceses across India — including Bangalore, Delhi, Agra, Mysore, Varanasi, Bareilly, and Assam — and in the Archdioceses of Cologne and Trier in Germany, as well as in Poland, the homeland of our foundress.

Satyaseva Sisters today — serving across three countries
Vision

Christ known, in every home

We envision families rooted in the living Word of God — where faith is not confined to Sunday, but carried gently into the kitchen, the sickbed, and the doorway of every house we visit.

Mission

To live and share the Truth

To dedicate ourselves wholly to God in the spirit of our foundress — bringing evangelization and catechetical formation directly into family life, and serving the needs of the poor, the aged, the orphaned, and the young wherever our communities are sent.

Why This Matters Now

Faith carried home

In a world where family life is increasingly rushed, scattered, and screen-lit, the Satyaseva charism — walking into a household, sitting at its table, and teaching the faith there rather than waiting for people to come to church — remains quietly countercultural.

From catechizing new converts in rural Karnataka to visiting elderly parishioners in German cities, our sisters bring Christ's presence not in grand spectacle but in the gentle ministry of daily encounter.

Prayers & Offerings

Pray with us

These prayers unite our congregation and the families we serve in daily worship.

Our Father

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be thy name; thy kingdom come, thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

Hail Mary

Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou amongst women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

For Our Families

Lord, bless the families we serve. Grant them faith to weather every storm, patience to nurture every child, and love to sanctify every home. Through the intercession of St. Angela Merici and our foundress Mother Regina, guide our sisters to bring Your truth to every doorstep. Amen.

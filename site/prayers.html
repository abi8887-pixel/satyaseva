🙏 Community In Prayer

Across our 32 convent stations in India, Germany, and Poland, the Satyaseva Catechist Sisters unite every evening to pray for:

"For all families undergoing spiritual trials, sickness, or separation — that Christ may heal their hearts and restore peace."

— Generalate Community Prayer

"For children, youth, and catechists receiving the Holy Sacraments in rural parish missions."

— Mission Convents Intention

"For vocations to the religious life and generous hearts willing to serve God's truth."

— Novitiate House, Bengaluru

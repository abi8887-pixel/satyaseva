Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Satyaseva Catechist Sisters of the Families (SCS)

1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

The Satyaseva Catechist Sisters of the Families ("SCS", "we", "our") are dedicated to serving families with integrity and trust. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to safeguarding any information you share with us through this website in compliance with international privacy laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Zero Third-Party Tracking & Cookies

We believe in quiet, respectful technology. This website:

  • Does not use tracking cookies or marketing pixels.
  • Does not collect behavioral advertising profiles.
  • Does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

3. Information We Collect and Why

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us:

  • Prayer Intentions & Inquiries: When you send us an email or submit a prayer request, we use your name and contact information solely to respond to your request and keep your intention in our prayers.
  • Station Updates (Congregation Members): Authorized sisters submitting reports, chronicles, or photos provide identification for archival and administrative records.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For visitors from the European Union (Germany, Poland), our processing of any voluntary communications is based on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) and explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) when you initiate contact.

5. Data Storage & Security

Information submitted through our administrative systems is stored in secure, encrypted environments with strict role-based access controls. We apply standard security practices to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data.
  • Withdraw your consent to communication at any time.

7. Contact the Data Controller

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact the Generalate:
